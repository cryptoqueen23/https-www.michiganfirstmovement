Our Mission

Take back our great state.

The Michigan First Movement is a grassroots patriot organization built on the unshakeable conviction that strong, high-functioning local communities form the foundation of a strong state and nation.

Because the power belongs to We the People.

01

Decentralize Power

We reject the growing expansion of federal tyranny that threatens state sovereignty and burdens Michigan families with an out-of-touch, unaccountable central government.

02

Uproot Corruption

Transform government into a lean, highly effective agency that puts the people of Michigan first before special interests, foreign powers, and unelected bureaucracy.

03

Protect Faith and Freedom

Protect founding principles and traditional values while freeing local communities to make their own decisions.

04

Empower Citizens

From taxes and debt to energy, infrastructure, election security, ballot access, and government waste, every cause advances citizen empowerment.

05

Mobilize Patriots

Through grassroots activism, education, community organizing, town halls, citizen initiatives, ballot referendums, and local solutions, we unite Michigan patriots.

06

Reclaim Michigan

Farmers, factory workers, small business owners, parents, veterans, and believers share a sacred duty to reclaim our state for We the People.

This is Michigan

Worth fighting for.

From the Great Lakes to family farms, factory floors, small towns, cities, and the Upper Peninsula, the movement begins with the people who call Michigan home.
