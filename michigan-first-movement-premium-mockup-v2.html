Power belongs to We the People.

The Michigan First Movement is a grassroots patriot organization built on the unshakeable conviction that strong, high-functioning local communities form the foundation of a strong state and nation.

Michigan First Movement

Faith. Family. Integrity. Hard work.

We believe that self-sufficient communities governed by constitutional principles, traditional American Christian values, and the principles of faith, family, integrity, and hard work are the only way to secure a prosperous future for Michigan and America.

  • ✓Strong local communities. Government should remain close to the people it serves.
  • ✓Constitutional principles. Liberty, accountability, and state sovereignty must be protected.
  • ✓Traditional values. Faith, family, personal responsibility, and hard work strengthen Michigan.
  • ✓Citizen power. The people, not unelected authorities, must shape Michigan’s future.
